# Make sanitized feeds publicly readable

Marketplace, Buffet, and Lost & Found feeds will be readable without authentication so community information can reach people before they create accounts.
Posting, commenting, claims, alerts, Gems, and Redemptions require an authenticated Participant.
Public lost-property content must use Moderator-sanitized descriptions and exclude Ownership Evidence, contact details, private profiles, Gem balances, selected NUS Zones, and activity histories.
